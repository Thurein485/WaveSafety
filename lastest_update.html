UNDERSTAND THE LEVELS

Rainfall Intensity Guide

Low

Continue monitoring the weather.

Moderate

Check local forecasts and prepare essentials.

High

Follow official warnings and avoid flood-prone areas.

Very high

Follow emergency instructions from local authorities.

These are general guidance categories, not official rainfall thresholds. Actual risk depends on local conditions and official warnings.

REMEMBER

Rainfall data is only one part of flood safety.

Stay alert to local conditions and follow official emergency instructions, even when the rainfall indicator is low.
