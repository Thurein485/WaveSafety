DISASTER RISK REDUCTION

Are you ready before the waters rise?

Floods can affect homes, farmlands, and communities with little notice. Understanding flood risks and taking action early helps protect lives and reduce damage.

“A flood doesn't wait. Neither should your preparation.”

Prepare an emergency bag, check official weather updates, and identify safe places in your community before flooding begins.

Recognize

Learn about local flood risks, heavy rainfall, and river warnings before the rainy season.

Prepare

Pack clean water, food, medicine, flashlights, and important documents in an emergency bag.

Protect

Move valuables to higher places and follow official evacuation instructions when necessary.

QUICK SAFETY TIPS

Small Actions.
Big Difference.

Stay alert, stay prepared, and stay safe. Here are some simple ways to prepare for a flood.

TIP 01

Stay Informed

Follow official weather forecasts and flood warnings.

TIP 02

Pack Supplies

Prepare drinking water, food, medicine, and flashlights.

TIP 03

Protect Your Home

Move important documents and valuables to higher places.

TIP 04

Help Each Other

Check on children, older people, and neighbors who may need help.

TIP 05

Make a Plan

Choose a safe meeting place and plan evacuation routes.

TIP 06

Charge Devices

Keep phones and power banks charged for emergency updates.
