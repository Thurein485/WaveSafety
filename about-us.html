Our Mission

Our mission is to raise awareness about flood risks and provide people with useful information to help them prepare for floods and stay safe during emergencies.

Our Vision

We envision communities that are better informed, prepared, and resilient against flooding through accessible knowledge and awareness.

Who We Are?

We are a team of students who want to make flood safety information easier to understand and access. Through WaveSafety, we aim to help communities learn about flood risks, recognise warning signs, and understand important safety measures.
